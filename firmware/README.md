# FW History

This version includes the improvements suggested by users on the Atarige [forum](https://forums.atariage.com/topic/351546-a8picocart-unocart-on-a-raspberry-pi-pico/). ​

## 11/03/2026

Support for new cartridges: (@ziggystar)
 - 42 Atarimax 1 MB Flash cartridge (old)
 - 75 Atarimax 1 MB Flash cartridge (new)
 - 61 MegaMax 2 MB cartridge
 - 63 Flash MegaCart 4 MB cartridge

Fix Joy 2B+ (@manterola)

## 09/02/2026

Support for new cartridges: (@ascrnet)
 - 53 Low bank 8kb cartridge to emulate Monkey Wrench II XL.
 - 104-108 J(Atari)Cart 8Kb to 128kb a new multi purpose cartridge from 2023.
 - 160 JRC6 64kb cartridge utilities PLOTTER CART. 

Update PicoSDK 2.3.0 (@ascrnet)

Altirraos updated to 3.10 (@ascrnet)

ATR density detection has been improved to detect the four most important types: SD, ED, D, and DS. (@ascrnet)
